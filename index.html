About Power Lab

Power Lab at Texas State University develops computational methods and practical solutions for the operation, planning, control, and resilience of modern power grids.

Our work addresses the increasing integration of inverter-based resources, renewable generation, energy storage, electric vehicles, and large flexible loads into the electric grid.

We combine physics-based power-system models with data-driven and optimization techniques to enable the next generation of reliable and intelligent grids.
